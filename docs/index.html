01 / About

Curious about how things work—and how to make them useful.

I work where semiconductor physics, technology development, and software meet. Since January 2026, I have coordinated technology-development projects for semiconductor sensor solutions while continuing my PhD in Si/SiGe spin-qubit research.

My work includes process integration, device and material characterisation, statistical analysis, automation, and project coordination. I like learning from colleagues, making complicated problems easier to understand, and building tools that are useful beyond a single task.
